3. Rebuild the migration sequence

  1. Abraham: Mesopotamia toward Canaan — covenant and promise.
  2. Famine: Canaan to Egypt — survival.
  3. Exodus: Egypt across Sinai toward Canaan — escape from slavery under Moses.
  4. Conquest: northern Israel to Assyrian control; Judah/Jerusalem to Babylon — forced displacement and captivity.
  5. Return: Babylon toward Jerusalem after Persia conquered Babylon — Cyrus permitted return.
