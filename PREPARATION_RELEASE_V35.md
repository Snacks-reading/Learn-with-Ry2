## Important repairs

Water and sunlight are abiotic; soil includes both nonliving material and biological contents. Photosynthesis stores energy in sugars and releases oxygen; ecosystems do not manufacture a supply of water. Zebras mainly graze while giraffes browse. Energy does not return to producers in a closed loop. Consumer level depends on the particular feeding pathway. Freshwater organisms do not become marine because of an incorrect heading. Predator removal can have conditional effects; it does not guarantee collapse of every organism.
